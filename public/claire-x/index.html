[ 01 ]

Personalized Second‑Order Scaffolding

Designing scalable, personalized scaffolding that moves beyond immediate task performance to develop durable, transferable advanced human competencies (AHCs), such as reasoning, critical thinking, argumentation, and problem-solving.

Guiding Question

How can personalized second-order scaffolding be pedagogically, methodologically, and technologically designed at scale, adapting to learners’ evolving needs and fading as competence grows, so that learners regulate their own learning and transfer AHCs across contexts?

Optimizing Human-AI Collaboration

Investigating how learners and AI combine complementary capabilities to reason and co-construct knowledge, with AI as an adaptive intellectual partner that preserves learner agency and independent thinking.

Guiding Question

How can second-order scaffolding principles (contingency, fading, and transfer of responsibility) regulate AI support and distribute cognitive responsibility between learners and AI over time, so that AHCs develop and transfer across contexts?

Generative AI Literacy

Supporting and examining how students and teachers progressively develop GenAI literacy: the knowledge, skills, and judgment needed for critical, ethical, and responsible GenAI use.

Guiding Question

How can personalized learning and assessment systems support the progressive development and transfer of students’ and teachers’ GenAI literacy, responding to their evolving competencies over time?

Methodology

We bridge learning theory, empirical evidence, and technological innovation to design and validate personalized learning and assessment environments that work at scale to solve real-world education challenges.

01

Design-Based Research

Iterative cycles of designing, deploying, and refining in authentic classrooms.

We treat the intervention and the knowledge about it as one integrated process: build, deploy with real students in real courses, study what the evidence reveals, and revise.

In Practice
  • Theory-driven design decisions
  • Deployment in authentic courses
  • Evidence-led revision cycles
02

Mixed Methods

Qualitative depth, quantitative rigor, together.

Learning outcomes are read alongside process data, and qualitative insight explains what numbers alone cannot: how students actually reason, struggle, and grow.

What We Combine
  • Outcome and process data together
  • Validated across diverse populations
  • Depth that explains the numbers
03

Learning Analytics

Tracing learning processes at scale to generate actionable evidence.

Rich process data, from behavioral traces and interaction logs to performance data, reveal how learning unfolds and how competencies develop and transfer over time, and findings feed directly back into redesign, tightening the loop between evidence and intervention.

What We Trace
  • Longitudinal learning trajectories
  • Engagement to higher-order thinking
  • Evidence that drives redesign
04

Human-Centered & Evidence-Centered Design

Human values and valid evidence at the core of every system.

Support is calibrated to the whole learner, cognitive and affective, not surface performance alone, and every system is held to one standard: learners who reason at a qualitatively higher level, independently, and transfer what they learn to new contexts.

What We Protect
  • Whole-learner modeling
  • Productive struggle preserved
  • Support that transfers to independence

Broader Impacts

Research in service of human potential: evidence that reaches beyond the lab, into classrooms, institutions, and society.

I.

Higher Education & Adult Learning

We design and validate scalable, AI-augmented learning and assessment systems for real-world challenges, moving beyond one-size-fits-all models toward support personalized to each learner’s knowledge and needs. Refined in authentic classrooms, our work spans disciplines from computer science, physics, and biology to writing and language learning.

II.

Researchers & Practitioners

An interdisciplinary hub at the intersection of AI and the learning sciences, the lab provides meaningful research and training opportunities for the next generation of researchers and practitioners. Our collaborations span academic institutions, government, industry, and educational organizations, generating transferable design principles for supporting learning.

III.

Academia & the Workforce

As AI disrupts the skills humans value, we advance systems that strengthen rather than outsource human capacities, preserving productive struggle and independent reasoning. We cultivate adaptable, whole-person lifelong learners, prepared to learn, question, reason, collaborate, and act responsibly across educational, professional, and societal contexts.

About the Lab

Curious?
We're here to share.

An interdisciplinary research hub at the intersection of learning sciences, artificial intelligence, and human-centered design, advancing the science of learning through theory-driven, AI-augmented educational systems that support the development and transfer of advanced human competencies.

Learners need advanced human competencies (higher-order, ethical, and 21st-century) that they can regulate, adapt, and transfer across contexts and throughout life. Yet instruction mostly supports immediate task performance, and the second-order scaffolding that builds independent learning and transfer is impractical in large classes. Closing that gap rigorously, at scale, is one of the most consequential open problems in the learning sciences.

Most systems adapt to surface signals, response accuracy, time-on-task, clickstreams, without ever modeling the learner’s reasoning or the processes behind their errors. That is differentiation by outcome, not personalization. True personalization requires a continuously updated, evidence-grounded model of each learner’s knowledge, reasoning, and engagement, and support that fades as competence grows, shifting responsibility to the learner.

Because a system can only support what it can infer. Assessment asks what a student knows; a learning environment must ask how they are reasoning and what support would advance it. The quality of any personalized system is bounded by the validity of its model of the learner.

Technologies change rapidly; the principles governing how people learn, how motivation shapes cognition, and what it means to grow as a thinker are far more durable. Our program is anchored to that durability, using deep knowledge of human learning to hold technology accountable to educational purposes.

Not a contest for dominance, but a meeting of strengths. AI will not replace humans, it gives human potential a farther reach, serving as connective tissue that amplifies our capacity to achieve what neither could alone.
